# Visual handoff

## Start with the story

Read the complete canonical story and evidence ledger before choosing a format. Identify zero to three visual opportunities using:

- information gain;
- importance to the story;
- verifiability;
- distinct role;
- brand fit.

If no relationship is worth visualizing, create only a cover when it has a clear communication purpose. Do not fill a quota.

## Visual brief contract

Every visual brief must contain:

```yaml
role: cover | portrait_card | quote_card | explainer | diagram | poster
purpose: ""
core_message: ""
channel: ""
aspect_ratio: ""
safe_zone: ""
evidence_ids: []
rights_status: confirmed | limited | unknown | not_required
brand_sources: []
must_include: []
must_avoid: []
method: generation | programmatic | layout | mixed
review_status: draft | reviewed | approved
```

## Method selection

- Use generation for atmosphere, editorial illustration, concepts, and non-literal scenes.
- Use programmatic drawing for exact data, diagrams, timelines, labels, and relationships.
- Use layout for Chinese text, quotations, dates, logos, QR codes, and fixed brand components.
- Prefer a mixed method: generated or sourced image base plus deterministic text and brand layout.

## People and places

- Use a real portrait only when the correct asset and intended-use permission are recorded.
- Never generate a likeness to replace a missing portrait.
- Do not fabricate an identifiable city landmark, policy scene, workplace, or event and present it as documentary evidence.
- Label editorial illustrations and conceptual diagrams when a reader could mistake them for factual records.

## Quality gates

Check:

- the image expresses the actual story;
- all numbers, dates, names, quotations, and labels match approved evidence;
- the official logo is a supplied asset, correctly proportioned, and not model-generated;
- Chinese and English text are legible and accurate;
- crop safety works for the target channel;
- the visual matches the current brand system;
- portrait, supplied-photo, logo, and location rights are clear;
- the asset remains `draft` until human review.
