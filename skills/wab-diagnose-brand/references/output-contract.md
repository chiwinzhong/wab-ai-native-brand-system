# Output contract

## Human-facing assessment

Use this order:

1. **Decision in scope** — one sentence.
2. **Primary bottleneck** — the constraint and why it comes first.
3. **Evidence boundary** — what is confirmed, public, claimed, conflicting, or missing.
4. **Six-lens assessment** — score, evidence, and implication for each lens.
5. **Differentiation hypothesis** — one statement plus the four-gate result.
6. **30-day path** — actions, owners, evidence, and decision gates.
7. **Do not automate** — consequential tasks reserved for people.
8. **Recommended route** — self-serve, professional diagnosis, or enterprise system.
9. **Next action** — one action and one accountable human.

## Tone

- Be direct, specific, and respectful.
- Recognize existing strengths before diagnosing the constraint.
- Avoid consultant filler, AI hype, academic overclaiming, and punitive language.
- Explain uncertainty in plain language.
- Prefer “the current evidence supports” over “the brand is”.

## Structured output

Use `brand-assessment.schema.json` when the assessment must be machine-readable. Keep human-facing prose consistent with the JSON record.

Never place hidden reasoning or private source content in the structured output. Record only conclusions, evidence references, limitations, and actions that may be audited or shared.
