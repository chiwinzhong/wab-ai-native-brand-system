# Interview-to-Story — WAB exploratory diagnosis

> Organization-authorized field exploration based only on 《即莞来》 and 《选择东莞》. No raw interview or unpublished case content is disclosed.

## Decision in scope

Decide whether the public proposition should lead with transcription, content repurposing, or an evidence-aware system that turns interviews into multilingual text and visual assets for different platforms.

## Primary bottleneck

**Proof and credibility** is now the constraint.

The earlier description was too broad because it presented organization-wide governance as the case. The corrected proposition is much easier to understand and buy:

> WAB Interview-to-Story turns interview audio or notes into an evidence-linked canonical story, then derives natural multilingual platform copy and brand-ready visuals while keeping facts, quotations, rights, and publication under human control.

The public package now contains a real Skill structure, a schema, a validator, negative tests, and a fictional example. What remains missing is a privacy-safe field comparison that shows the quality of the transformation without publishing a real person's raw material.

## Evidence boundary

- Only the two approved editorial patterns—《即莞来》 and 《选择东莞》—are used as field evidence.
- The person route is supported by a formal sequence from interview source through transcript, diagnosis, direction, canonical story, fact and quotation review, bilingual adaptation, and visual package.
- The place/choice route is supported by a separate rule: current public information is primary, while interview material is bounded context.
- Both routes preserve a Chinese factual mother text, natural English re-editing, visual production after content approval, and final human publication authority.
- The public repository does not include raw audio, full transcripts, named unpublished cases, portraits, private source maps, internal task counts, organization-wide service lines, or account access.
- No outcome or efficiency claim is independently validated.

## Differentiation hypothesis

> Unlike transcription and content-repurposing tools, WAB Interview-to-Story establishes one evidence-linked canonical story before deriving multilingual platform copy and visual assets, using different evidence rules for person-led and place/choice stories.

| Gate | Result | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| True | Pass | The two field routes and public Skill contract implement the sequence. |
| Relevant | Partial | Fragmented interview production is a plausible pain point, but prospective-user interviews are still needed. |
| Ownable | Partial | The combination is distinct, but a documented alternative comparison has not been published. |

## 30-day path

1. Select one first commercial audience and one trigger event, such as a founder interview, member story, city-choice story, or executive thought-leadership program.
2. Produce one synthetic end-to-end sample and one redacted example for each field route.
3. Compare the Skill with a prompt-only baseline using the same source packet, model, target channels, and time budget.
4. Measure unsupported claims, quote drift, source traceability, human corrections, cross-channel contradictions, and visual rework.
5. Invite an English editor and an external operator to audit the package and publish the limitations.

## Human decisions

- Zhiying Zhong approves the public product positioning and the bounded field claim.
- The organization approves every redaction and any use of a series name, article fragment, quotation, portrait, logo, or visual asset.
- The interviewee or authorized rights holder approves the applicable text, quotation, portrait, and platform uses.
- A language reviewer approves target-language meaning before the derivative is described as approved.
- A named account owner authorizes the exact publication action.

## Do not automate

- Do not infer quotations, facts, identities, current policy, or publication rights.
- Do not force a person story into city promotion or use one person as group evidence.
- Do not let an interview replace current authoritative sources in a place/choice story.
- Do not turn a natural English adaptation into a stronger claim than the Chinese factual mother text.
- Do not fabricate a person, place, event, logo, or policy fact inside a visual.
- Do not publish from a completed package without explicit account-level authorization.

## Recommended route

`enterprise_system`

The open Skill can run one bounded package. Organizations that want recurring interview production need their own editorial routes, source systems, brand assets, permissions, review roles, feedback loop, and account governance. Those are implementation decisions, not features that an installation can safely infer.
