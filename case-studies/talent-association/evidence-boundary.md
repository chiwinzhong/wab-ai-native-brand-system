## Public-safe now

- the two series names and their high-level editorial distinction;
- the generalized interview-to-story workflow;
- evidence, quotation, translation, visual, rights, and publication gates;
- the existence of bilingual and visual production patterns;
- the open Skill, schema, validator, tests, and fictional package;
- the limitations and next evaluation design.

## Requires explicit approval before publication

- any raw recording, transcript excerpt, direct quotation, unpublished article, or identifiable case detail;
- any portrait, supplied photograph, voice sample, logo, QR code, brand template, or location-specific image;
- any claim about output volume, time saved, reach, conversion, participant response, or organizational effect;
- any real platform screenshot, link, account, schedule, or publication record;
- any commercial relationship, partner reference, or endorsement.

## Excluded from this public case

- organization-wide content lines, service architecture, source indexes, task inventories, internal paths, governance files, and unpublished plans;
- names or identities of unpublished interviewees, members, staff, partners, or service recipients;
- contact data, private messages, meeting records, credentials, account settings, or payment information;
- hidden prompts, private reasoning, or the complete internal editorial system;
- claims that the Skill replaces editorial, translation, legal, privacy, rights, public-affairs, or publication responsibility.
