# Daddy Asks — WAB exploratory diagnosis

> Owner-authorized field exploration. System evidence is inspectable by the owner; performance evidence is not independently reviewed.

## Decision in scope

Decide whether the international public case should lead with parenting-content scale, AI efficiency, or the conversion of founder judgment into a governed and reusable knowledge system.

## Primary bottleneck

**Proof and credibility** is the current constraint.

Daddy Asks already has substantial operating structure: formal sources, rules, modules, routing, review gates, and reusable Skill assets. The public evidence layer is weaker. Exact efficiency, audience, inquiry, and revenue figures are still management-reported, and the article index contains one count conflict.

Leading with large numbers or productivity claims would therefore make the case easier to market but harder to trust. The first public claim should be narrower: the project has made selected founder judgment versioned, reviewable, and reusable.

## Evidence boundary

- Project ownership, operating rules, workflow artifacts, module structure, and Skill assets are supported by owner-authorized records.
- The public-safe article count remains “more than 100” / “100+”; the owner overview now records 141 effective articles (2026-09-12), but exact counts stay out of promotional claims.
- Time saving, reach, inquiries, collaboration interest, and revenue are management claims pending redacted source review.
- No controlled comparison establishes AI as the cause of any audience or business result.
- No child identity, family image, consultation transcript, or private message is used.
