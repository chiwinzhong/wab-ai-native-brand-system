## Public-safe now

- project ownership and scope;
- “more than 100 formal articles”;
- the existence of formal rules, modules, routing, Skill assets, review gates, and version history;
- a description of the workflow and human authority boundaries;
- the fact that performance metrics are management-reported and awaiting verification.

## Requires owner approval before publication

- any platform screenshot, even after redaction;
- exact follower, reach, consultation, collaboration, revenue, or time-saving figures;
- any child or family story not already approved for this specific use;
- quotations from private messages or consultations;
- partner, advertiser, school, location, or family identifiers.

## Never include in the public case

- child names, faces, schools, precise locations, private health information, or account identifiers;
- consultation transcripts or personally identifiable reader questions;
- passwords, tokens, account access details, unpublished family records, or hidden system prompts;
- claims that the system provides medical, psychological, legal, or professional parenting approval.
