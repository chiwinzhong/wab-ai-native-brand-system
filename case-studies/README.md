# Exploratory field cases

These cases show how WAB behaves outside a fully synthetic example. They are evidence-bounded learning records, not testimonials or business-impact claims.

| Case | Ownership | What it explores | Evidence status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Daddy Asks](daddy-asks/README.md) | Founder-owned project | Turning recurring human judgment into governed, reusable content and Skill workflows | Owner-authorized system evidence; performance metrics awaiting source verification |
| [Interview-to-Story](talent-association/README.md) | Two organization-authorized editorial patterns | Turning interview audio or notes into evidence-linked multilingual text and visual assets through the 《即莞来》 and 《选择东莞》 routes | Workflow evidence and runnable Skill contract; no raw interviews or outcome claims disclosed |

Each case must separate confirmed operating artifacts from management-reported outcomes, disclose conflicts and gaps, remove unnecessary personal data, and retain named human approval gates.
