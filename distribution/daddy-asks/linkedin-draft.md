# LinkedIn draft

AI did not write my parenting voice. It made part of my judgment executable.

I started the Chinese parenting project *爸爸每事问* (*Daddy Asks*) from real family moments. I wrote the first ten essays myself—not simply to establish a tone, but to establish judgment:

- what can be observed without pretending to know a child’s inner state;
- how to hold emotion without removing boundaries;
- when the parent must examine his own reaction;
- what must remain private;
- what always requires human publication approval.

Over time, the project grew into more than 100 formal articles, versioned writing and channel rules, a structured article source, topic modules, a question router, review gates, Skill specifications, and a discoverable Codex Skill.

The important shift was not:

`human writing → AI writing`

It was:

`lived experience → founder judgment → correction → approved rule → reusable capability`

When I ran the project through WAB, my evidence-aware brand-diagnosis Skill, the primary bottleneck was not production. It was proof.

The operating system is more mature than the public evidence layer. Time savings, reach, reader questions, collaboration interest, and early revenue remain management-reported until I complete a redacted source review and a controlled comparison.

So the claim is deliberately narrow:

> A founder-owned content project has converted part of its recurring editorial judgment into a versioned, reviewable, and reusable AI operating system.
