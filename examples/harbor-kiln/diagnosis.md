# Harbor Kiln — synthetic WAB diagnosis

> This is a fictional example. It contains no real customer, market, or performance evidence.

## Decision in scope

Decide whether Harbor Kiln should lead the next sales cycle with sustainability, design flexibility, or operating durability.

## Primary bottleneck

The strongest current claim is also the least supported one. Harbor Kiln describes itself as the most sustainable option, but the synthetic public record does not define or prove that comparison. Producing more content around it would scale credibility risk.

The more concrete opportunity is operating continuity: batch matching, replacement, and visual consistency for independent restaurants.

## Evidence boundary

- The founder's sustainability position is a management claim, not a confirmed comparative fact.
- Product pages visibly describe batch replacement and glaze matching.
- Synthetic buyer interviews suggest replacement reliability matters.
- Repairability, competitor difference, and commercial impact remain unverified.
