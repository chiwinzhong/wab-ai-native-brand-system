# Contributing

WAB welcomes critique that makes the open diagnostic layer more evidence-aware, useful, and governable.

## Useful contributions

- a reproducible failure case using synthetic or authorized data;
- a clearer evidence classification or diagnostic boundary;
- contract-validator improvements;
- cross-agent compatibility findings;
- translations that preserve the decision and safety meaning;
- evaluation methods that do not overclaim business impact.

## Before opening an issue

- remove customer names, private documents, account credentials, and confidential evidence;
- describe one bounded decision and the expected versus actual behavior;
- state the agent or runtime and version used;
- distinguish a factual error from a strategic disagreement;
- do not submit proprietary WAB materials obtained outside this repository.

By contributing, you confirm that you have the right to share the submitted material. Commercial terms, customer work, and private WAB operating methods are handled outside the public issue tracker.
