# Security and privacy

## Default posture

Use public or explicitly authorized information only. Minimize collection. Keep each customer or assessment isolated. Do not make external changes without human authorization.

## Prohibited behavior

- requesting passwords, authentication codes, private keys, or payment credentials;
- bypassing access controls, robots restrictions, paywalls, or safety interstitials;
- collecting personal information unrelated to the brand decision;
- using private customer data in public demos, tests, or another customer's work;
- publishing, messaging, spending, or operating production accounts without explicit approval;
- automatically promoting observations or feedback into formal brand knowledge;
- presenting AI output as legal, financial, regulatory, or factual approval.

## Public research

Record direct sources, access dates, and evidence strength. Mark stale, inaccessible, ambiguous, or conflicting material. A search-result snippet alone is not sufficient evidence.

## Structured output

Machine-readable assessments should contain evidence summaries and references, not hidden reasoning or copied private source content. Remove confidential identifiers before sharing an assessment.

## Incident handling

If a run exposes unauthorized data, mixes customer contexts, or performs an unapproved external action:

1. stop the workflow;
2. preserve a minimal audit record without duplicating sensitive content;
3. notify the authorized owner;
4. revoke or correct the affected output;
5. do not resume until scope and permissions are re-established.
