# Architecture

## Design goal

Convert brand work from ungoverned generation into a sequence of auditable decisions that can later feed reusable organizational capability.

## Open diagnostic layer

The open Skill performs no production-account writes and stores no customer data by itself. It coordinates:

1. scope and authority check;
2. bounded intake;
3. evidence classification;
4. public-signal research when authorized and available;
5. six-lens diagnosis;
6. primary-bottleneck selection;
7. differentiation-gate test;
8. 30-day action design;
9. human-approval and automation boundaries;
10. structured handoff.

## Professional layer

The professional layer is operated by WAB and may add proprietary question selection, management interviews, evidence reconciliation, report production, human review, and facilitation. It is not included in the open repository.

## Enterprise layer

The enterprise layer may include governed brand knowledge, role and permission models, versioned decisions, content and visual execution units, review gates, feedback classification, and controlled knowledge updates.

## State separation

Do not mix these records:

- preliminary open assessment;
- professional client diagnosis;
- one-off task record;
- approved enterprise brand knowledge.

A public observation never becomes approved enterprise knowledge without the required human confirmation and WAB review.

## Human authority

Keep these decisions human-controlled:

- confirmation of company and product facts;
- positioning and major differentiation choices;
- regulated or legal claims;
- budget, pricing, contract, and publishing decisions;
- permissions and access to private data;
- acceptance of feedback as reusable knowledge;
- final approval of client-facing or public output.
