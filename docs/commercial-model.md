# Commercial model

## Open core

The open Skill demonstrates the WAB point of view and creates a useful first result: an evidence-aware decision brief. It is designed to earn trust through inspectable method, conservative claims, structured output, and failure boundaries.

## Professional diagnosis

Commercial value begins where generic self-serve analysis becomes unreliable:

- evidence is private, contradictory, or strategically sensitive;
- the real issue requires management interviews;
- a positioning decision affects budget or organizational direction;
- the organization needs a client-ready report and facilitated decision;
- legal, copyright, regulatory, or reputational risk requires review.

The professional service sells judgment, evidence reconciliation, facilitation, and accountable quality—not access to a prompt.

## Enterprise system

Enterprise value comes from repeated capability:

- governed knowledge instead of scattered files;
- explicit decision owners and review gates;
- reusable positioning, content, and visual rules;
- production workflows that retain human authority;
- feedback that is classified before becoming knowledge;
- a system that improves across tasks without silently rewriting history.

## Conversion principle

Do not force every user into a sales path. Recommend professional or enterprise scope only when the assessment reveals a material reason. The diagnosis itself must remain useful even when no commercial engagement follows.

## Public proof roadmap

Only claim evidence actually collected:

1. package validation and deterministic tests;
2. synthetic end-to-end cases;
3. authorized field trials with explicit boundaries;
4. anonymized failure and revision reports;
5. repeat-use and human-edit metrics;
6. independently reviewable case evidence.

Do not turn downloads, stars, or generated output into proof of business impact.
