# Evaluation protocol

WAB should be evaluated as a decision and governance system, not by how much text it generates.

## Unit of evaluation

One run covers one brand, one business scope, and one consequential decision. Do not combine unrelated brands or decisions to improve apparent coverage.

## Contract checks

Every valid assessment must:

- separate facts, public observations, claims, signals, hypotheses, conflicts, and gaps;
- cite only evidence IDs present in the ledger;
- assess exactly six lenses on a 0–3 rubric;
- select one primary bottleneck;
- test one differentiation hypothesis through four gates;
- include human decisions and a do-not-automate list;
- choose one supported commercial route;
- state limitations.

## Field-test measures

For authorized real cases, record these separately:

1. **Evidence correction rate** — material evidence items corrected by an authorized owner.
2. **Decision revision rate** — primary bottlenecks changed after expert or management review.
3. **Unsupported-claim rate** — public or client-facing claims removed because evidence was inadequate.
4. **Action acceptance rate** — recommended 30-day actions accepted by the named decision-maker.
5. **Human edit rate** — substantive revisions required before the assessment is usable.
6. **Repeat-use rate** — later decisions that reuse approved evidence or rules without context loss.
7. **Failure disclosure** — cases where the Skill is degraded, blocked, or routed to professional review.

Do not combine these into a single vanity score. Downloads, stars, output volume, and model fluency are not evidence of brand or business impact.

## Comparison design

When practical, compare WAB with a baseline brief produced without the Skill. Keep the brand, decision, evidence packet, model, and time budget consistent. Have an authorized operator or blinded reviewer assess:

- evidence traceability;
- clarity of the primary decision;
- usefulness of the next action;
- unsupported claims;
- required human correction;
- explicit governance boundaries.

Report failed and inconclusive cases. Do not claim causality from a single organization, a short observation window, or self-reported satisfaction alone.

## Current evidence status

Version 0.1.0 has package validation, a synthetic end-to-end example, and deterministic positive and negative contract tests. It does not yet have authorized field results or independently reviewed business-impact evidence.
